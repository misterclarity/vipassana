Before you book

  • A single room is never fully guaranteed. Centres allocate rooms per course and per gender, and a full course can mean sharing. Every claim below is quoted from the centre's own page with a link — read the quote, then confirm with the centre when you apply.
  • 10-day courses are open to old students. They are the standard course old students re-sit; apply through the old-student application on the centre's page. Service periods are excluded here, since you asked to sit.
  • Language shown in grey is inferred from the centre's usual teaching language because the schedule row did not name one. Courses at centres outside the UK are commonly taught in the local language with English translation available — verify before applying.
  • Non-centre courses (rented venues in countries without a centre) are not listed: they are not published as per-centre schedules, and they almost always use shared accommodation. Check the official course search if you want those too.
